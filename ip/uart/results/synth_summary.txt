Notes:
- uart and uart_tx at 115200 baud, 8N1 did not meet 4.0 ns (uart WNS -0.005 ns, uart_tx WNS
  -0.140 ns). They are reported at 4.5 ns, where timing is met. The critical path of uart_tx
  was not analyzed; tx_ready_o is a combinational function of the counters (GitHub issue).
- WHS_reg is the hold slack between registers. The hold slack of the ports is an artifact of
  the out-of-context flow (I/O delays of 0 ns, Route 35-198 and 35-426 warnings).
- With PARITY=0 the parity accumulator of uart_rx is removed by synthesis (Synth 8-6014).
- ASYNC_REG_FF is the number of flip-flops with the ASYNC_REG property: 2 for the receiver
  synchronizer, 0 for the transmitter.
- Results are valid for these tags only; placement is not monotonic with the period.
