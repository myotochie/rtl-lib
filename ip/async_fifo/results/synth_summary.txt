async_fifo: synthesis and implementation summary

Command (cmd, after settings64.bat, from the repository root):
  vivado -mode batch -nolog -nojournal -source scripts/synth_vivado_async.tcl -tclargs <WIDTH> <DEPTH> <SYNC_STAGES> <PERIOD_NS>

Runs:
  ... -tclargs 8 16 2 4.5
  ... -tclargs 8 16 3 4.7
  ... -tclargs 32 1024 2 6.6

Setup: out-of-context, part xc7z010clg400-1, Vivado 2023.1, two clocks of the same period with
no relation between them, I/O delays 0 ns. Crossing constraints: set_max_delay -datapath_only,
one period between the clocks, SYNC_STAGES periods for the memory to rd_data_o paths.
Raw reports (utilization, timing, CDC, crossing paths) are in build/vivado/<run>/.

Reading the numbers:
- TIMING=MET: setup slack is not negative in both clock domains and on both crossings. WHS_reg is
  the worst hold slack between registers, positive in every run.
- The frequency is the highest one tried at which timing was met. It is not a maximum: lower periods
  were not explored.
- The placement is not monotonic with the period. 8x16 with SYNC_STAGES=3 did not meet timing at
  4.4 ns (WNS_rd = -0.183 ns) and met it at 4.7 ns. The failed run is not a result.
- ASYNC_REG_FF equals the expected count in every run: the synchronizer flip-flops keep their attribute.
- The hold slack on the ports and the HD.CLK_SRC and HD.PARTPIN_LOCS warnings come from the
  out-of-context mode with 0 ns input delays. They are not design issues and are not reported here.
- report_cdc: the Gray buses are recognized as synchronized with ASYNC_REG and
  Max Delay Datapath Only. Its CDC-6 warning (multi-bit) is expected for Gray buses.
